Advice for Students

Make the most of your diagnostic results with these tips from experienced physics educators.

  • ✓

    Start with the Foundation Tier to establish a baseline, even if you are a Year 11 student.

  • ✓

    Be honest — don’t guess randomly. Mark what you genuinely know so the diagnostic is accurate.

  • ✓

    Use the PDF report to structure your revision — focus on URGENT topics first, then REVIEW topics.

  • ✓

    Retake the diagnostic every 2–3 weeks to track your improvement and adjust your revision plan.

  • ✓

    Pair the diagnostic results with past paper practice for best results — use AQA past papers from 2018 onwards.

  • ✓

    Don’t just memorise — aim to understand the physics behind each answer. Examiners reward understanding.

  • ✓

    Share your results with your teacher or parent for targeted support in your weakest areas.
